I was wondering what is the counter word for kanji and I couldn't find a realiable answer online, so I asked my Japanese teacher, [絵理子先生](http://elcbkk.wixsite.com/erikojuku2016). The answer is surprisingly simple. One of those that when one sees them, one things to oneself, duh! Without further ado, for counting kanji in Japanese you can use {{< ruby "一" "ひと" >}}{{< ruby "文" "も" >}}{{< ruby "字" "じ" >}}、{{< ruby "二" "ふた" >}}{{< ruby "文" "も" >}}{{< ruby "字" "じ" >}} or {{< ruby "一" "いち" >}}{{< ruby "字" "じ" >}}、 {{< ruby "二" "に" >}}{{< ruby "字" "じ" >}}. Now, trick question. How do you count {{< ruby "感" "かん" >}}じ?
