[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) provides the smartest flashcards ever! Anki has a highly optimized algorithm that optimizes the use of a technique called [spaced repetition](http://www.gwern.net/Spaced%20repetition). For memories and facts to get “saved” to long term memory you have to recall that memory several times at different intervals, otherwise you forget it. Spaced repetition optimizes this process by showing you flashcards with perfect timing, so that you memorize words and facts with the least amount of reviews possible, and without forgetting them!

This technique can be used to learn Hiragana! How do you do it?

1. Visit [Anki’s website](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) and hit on the download button. That will take you to the bottom of the page where you can find the download link and instructions on how to install on Windows, Mac and Linux.
2. Download the Anki companion deck to the [Japanese Ammo Hiragana Guide](http://www.japaneseammo.com/the-easiest-way-to-learn-hiragana/). If you still haven’t checked out the guide, please do that first. You can find the deck at [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2136150393). AnkiWeb is a place where Anki users share decks with others. An Anki deck is pretty much the same a real life flashcard deck.
3. Fire up Anki and do the initial setup.
4. At the bottom of your Anki window click on import file and choose the deck you just downloaded.
5. Set the default number of new cards, in this case hiragana characters, that you want to see each day for this deck. You can do this by clicking on the gear icon next to the deck you just imported, called “Hiragana - Japanese Ammo”, and then selecting “Options”.
6. Click on the deck and then press “Study Now”.
7. When you see the Hiragana written in roomaji, write down the appropriate Hiragana in paper and then click on “Show Answer”. If you were not able to recall it click on “Again”, if you recalled it but made a tiny mistake or it was hard to recall press “Good”. If it was easy, then click on easy, it’s just that easy!
8. Keep repeating until you work through all your new cards and reviews of the day!

## Some Anki Tips

### Start with a moderate pace

Perhaps five per day, and then ramp it up if you want to do more or slow it down if you think its too much. If you do too many new cards a day you will get swamped with reviews in subsequent days. What are reviews? A review is when Anki shows you again a card that you had seen before as “new” to get you to recall your memory and thus push it down a bit further towards long term memory.

### Use Anki daily and in short spurts throughout the day

It’s the best way to build your memory. Doing it daily makes sure spaced repetition works its magic at its best and doing it in short spurts makes it easier to keep your full attention on the activity. If you try to do marathonic sessions you are bound to get bored and distracted.

### Make using Anki a habit

Make it something you have to absolutely do each day, just like brushing your teeth! Forming a habit out of it is the quickest way to success with Anki! That said, it’s also good to take a break from time to time.
